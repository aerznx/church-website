Who We Are

Light of Gospel is a vibrant congregation in Singapore standing firmly on classic apostolic Pentecostal doctrines. We preach the uncompromised Word of God, cultivating dynamic environments for spirit-filled worship, while ensuring local families stay securely anchored together in truth.

Our teaching and fellowship sessions are currently tailored in the Malayalam language to care for our community's roots. We are prayerfully expanding our scope and intend to establish multi-language services in the near future to seamlessly reach out to younger generations and diverse communities across Singapore.
